# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A single local desktop operator researching crypto markets, observing algorithm
decisions, tracking connected assets, and evaluating paper-trading performance.

## Product Purpose

An evidence-first research and paper-trading workstation. The unified terminal
answers market state, algorithm observations, intent, holdings, and performance.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Electron/React with CoquiClient contracts, centralized query polling and local
storage. Live order submission remains disabled. Connected portfolios and paper
simulations are distinct. Missing data, negative evidence and execution uncertainty
are explicit. The user requests one interface replacing Simple/Advanced modes.

## Brand Commitments

Coqui Crypto identity and green accent. The user-supplied trading-terminal
screenshot is the primary visual reference. Cryptocurrency vectors come from the
supplied Figma community pack.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing typed market, portfolio, decision, risk, research and performance channels.
Editable Figma specification: https://www.figma.com/design/oYzyZ6Spywty2LQ6CT3EBs

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable controls, high-contrast/light themes, reduced motion, explicit
status labels, responsive panels and scrollable dense financial tables.
