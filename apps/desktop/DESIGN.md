# Coqui terminal design

The editable Figma specification is the visual authority:
https://www.figma.com/design/oYzyZ6Spywty2LQ6CT3EBs

Near-black neutral surfaces, subtle charcoal dividers, compact 28–32px rows and
controls, 4–6px radii, IBM Plex Sans at 12–14px, tabular numerals, and restrained
green/red financial colors. Avoid decorative gradients, shadows and oversized
cards. Top navigation replaces the previous persistent left sidebar.

Overview combines chart/depth, book/trades, tabbed account/evidence data and an
algorithm sidebar. Below 1600px the sidebar becomes a drawer; below 1280px market
panels stack. Missing or stale values never become fabricated zero balances.

Coin vectors retain their original appearance and appear at 18–20px in asset rows.
Unknown assets use a symbol fallback. Market displays remain informational; the
algorithm sidebar presents completed-bar decisions with independent timestamps.
