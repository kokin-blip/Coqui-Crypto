## Adopt

### Performance references

- A dominant equity chart with an explicit passive benchmark.
- P&L calendar with day selection, drawdown history, worst-drawdown summaries, monthly returns,
  return distribution, seasonality, and clearly explained risk metrics.
- Negative months and failed studies remain visibly negative.
- Every chart and metric exposes its date range, calculation assumptions, exclusions, and immutable
  data identity.

### B00NO references

- Persistent left navigation, continuously visible operating state, dense structured tables,
  decision explanations, position detail, and useful filtering.
- Clear status grouping and compact row scanning where the underlying Coqui data supports it.

### Terminal reference

- Chart-first composition, source health, operational events, and a restrained workstation tone.

### Legend/Mobbin reference

- Short local transitions that preserve route and panel context, polished connection onboarding,
  stable focus, and clear pending/success/failure changes.

## Reject

- Meme-token discovery, Pump.fun, sniping, auto-buy, market making, live buy/sell, leverage,
  derivatives, transfers, withdrawals, and multi-exchange execution.
- Monochrome green, fake code, ticker decoration, excessive pills, dense unlabeled abbreviations,
  overcrowded position rows, cinematic financial controls, and continuously animated P&L.
- Copied Mobbin implementation details or recognizable third-party visual themes.
- Decorative charts or inferred values where Coqui has no verified historical evidence.

## Coqui-specific synthesis

The default canvas is near-black navy with dark-blue surfaces. Blue-violet identifies selection and
primary paper actions; mint, coral, and amber communicate positive, negative, and warning/stale
states alongside signs, labels, and shapes. The strategy warning and reason to stand down remain
above performance figures. Real and paper accounting stay visually adjacent where useful but are
never combined into one total.

Motion uses only the existing 83/167/250 ms tokens. Route and drawer entry, local status changes,
new fills, risk-check progression, calendar expansion, and connection onboarding may animate with
opacity/transform. Balances, P&L digits, historical curves, and unconfirmed fills do not animate.
