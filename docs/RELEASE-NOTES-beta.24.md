# Coqui 0.1.0-beta.24 — experimental macOS release

This release provides **macOS Apple Silicon (arm64) only**: DMG, ZIP and a SHA-256 manifest. It includes the current application updates through the small-portfolio roadmap and the previously local recovery/hourly-study work. Signing remains ad-hoc, without Developer ID notarization. Follow the [installation guide](INSTALL.md) and verify the download checksum.

Beta.22 and beta.23 were unpublished candidates. Clean-checkout startup and generated-provenance checks prevented their publication; this version supersedes them without moving either tag.

## Updates

- Capital-flow accounting separates contributions, withdrawals and investment results. Missing history, timing, fees or valuations produce explicit unavailable metrics.
- Small-portfolio budgets, venue constraints and feasibility checks support reserve previews, capital-aware replay and offline strategy/policy comparisons.
- Reviewed internal paper-reserve and sale capabilities preserve existing eligibility, authority, OMS, pause and recovery checks. Saving settings or previewing a consequence does not authorize a financial action.
- Prospective study tools separate approved design, elapsed coverage, development choice, untouched final evaluation and qualification. Historical algorithm experience is not fresh prospective evidence.
- The new account-authority journal is a **disabled foundation** with immutable preparation history, revision checks and retry protection. It creates no funded cash claims or broker permit. This Mac is the intended sole-host direction; actual account-wide enforcement and connected verification remain unfinished.
- Alpaca paper recovery includes bounded read retries, explicit rejection/unknown-outcome reconciliation, fee/position settlement evidence and authority-aware suspend/resume behavior. Temporary waits do not manufacture fees, accounting certainty or a new submission opportunity.
- Hourly shadow research has a separate read-only collection lane and an explicit reviewed study-restart flow. It submits no broker orders; publishing this release registers or starts no actual study.
- Activity views retain financial history independently of diagnostic volume, group repeated fee waits and explain profit metrics and unavailable evidence.
- Disabled/legacy startup no longer hashes compiled reserve artifacts eagerly. Artifact proof remains mandatory when a reserve operation actually needs it; missing artifacts never become a fabricated identity.
- Every desktop build first regenerates current workspace manifests. Packaged startup verification requires its source revision, compiled research revision and version to agree, and rejects dirty or stale generated evidence. Altered or untracked source still marks the build dirty.
- Zod is pinned to 4.6.6 to address the [high-severity collection-validation advisory](https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-mrhx-c7xc-m3w5). The patched production audit reports no known vulnerabilities in its recorded check.

## Scope and remaining gates

Live trading and external withdrawals remain disabled. Connected reserve authority, account/host/credential exclusivity, current financial/fee/native evidence, applicable human review and strategy qualification remain separate open gates. P5-H actual development research and P7-H–J approved future registration, genuine elapsed evidence, timely freeze, final-once evaluation and independent acceptance remain pending. No frozen/consumed evidence becomes fresh through installation or a source update.

Actual-market latency, representative installed-account restore, human VoiceOver review and other existing operational/review requirements remain open. Synthetic/native/package checks validate the software and artifact at their recorded scope; they do not certify trading performance. Windows and Intel Mac installers are not included.

Publishing the files does not install the app, migrate an actual profile, change credentials, assign a host, resume a campaign, place/cancel an order or grant research authority.

Verification, exact source and artifact receipts are recorded in [the release delivery](implementation/beta23-macos-release-2026-10-29.md).
