# Coqui Crypto 0.1.0-beta.14 — experimental

- Adds a unified trading terminal with market depth, recent trades, paper orders, and clearer research views.
- Improves parallel paper execution, reconciliation, recovery, and order safety reporting.
- Adds verified multi-venue market archive acquisition and stronger research integrity tooling.
- Adds an opt-in TrendVol research candidate for reducing relative tilts when momentum scores are close. It is not enabled as a default.

Live exchange trading remains disabled. Historical research features do not imply strategy profitability. macOS builds are ad-hoc signed; see `docs/INSTALL.md` for first-launch guidance.
