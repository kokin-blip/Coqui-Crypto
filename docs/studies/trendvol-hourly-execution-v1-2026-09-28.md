# TrendVol hourly execution v1 — prospective shadow study

`trendvol-hourly-execution-v1` changes **execution timing only**. The target is the existing stored TrendVol v4.2 decision from the prior completed UTC Coinbase daily bar: 120-day momentum, 30-day volatility and 100-day trend are unchanged. The operational Alpaca paper path retains its 00:00–00:15 UTC daily window and five 04:00–20:00 UTC four-hour windows. The hourly candidate has no Alpaca submission method or promotion switch.

## Frozen policy and registration

- First registered on an active paper tick; the study starts at the **next UTC midnight**. The profile-scoped append-only study record freezes its experiment ID, candidate version, policy and implementation hashes, exact dates, cost model, and starting-state convention before any study observation is collected. A new experiment, implementation, or parameter hash mismatch makes collection unavailable until a new version is registered; it does not change the existing study.
- Checks occur at 00:00–23:00 UTC, within each hour's first 15 minutes. The 00:00 check uses the existing 5% daily drift threshold; later checks use the existing 1% intraday threshold. Minimum trade is $25 after Alpaca quantity increments and minimum size. Every quote must be no more than 60 seconds old. Sells are planned before buys, with cash reserved for buys. A modeled order starts a 120-minute **per-asset** cooldown. Unresolved virtual quantity blocks another intent in that asset; the slot record contains intent, fill, remaining quantity, cost, and blocker reasons.
- The main process records current Alpaca US crypto bid/ask timestamps and current paper-asset rules for BTC, ETH and LTC, plus the stored decision day, target hash, and Coinbase dataset hash. Missing, stale, ineligible, or unfinished evidence cannot create a new target or a valid observation. No credentials, tokens, or raw API bodies enter the study records or renderer.
- The candidate owns an independent virtual book. The first observed slot records the actual paper account's cash and quantities as the study opening snapshot; the start of each development block and holdout records a separate, common opening snapshot for matched replay. These snapshots may already reflect a live daily order at 00:00, since the collector runs after the authoritative paper pass. Both replay policies use the same snapshot. Shadow market orders assume immediate fills; injected partial fills test pending/reconciliation mechanics but are **not** evidence of Alpaca liquidity.

## Matched evaluation

The read-only `node scripts/research-hourly-execution.mjs /path/to/coqui.db` report compares the current daily-plus-four-hour policy and the hourly candidate at identical recorded hourly quotes, completed daily targets, starting cash/holdings, asset rules, and modeled fills. It reports base costs and doubled friction. The cost model is 0.25% taker fee, observed half-spread, and 0.15% adverse slippage per side; buy fees reduce received crypto and sell fees reduce cash. Net return, max drawdown, turnover, fees, spread, slippage, order count, blocked and missed orders, and modeled partials are reported. Actual Alpaca partial-order IDs are counted separately; a virtual fill is never labeled as an Alpaca fill.

Development has three fixed chronological 20-day blocks; the untouched holdout is the following 30 days. The normal report does not read holdout observations. Explicit `--holdout` is allowed only after its recorded end. All 24 hourly observations are required for a comparable day. Missing days, including times when the desktop app is closed, are marked unavailable; a block with incomplete coverage has no aggregate return claim, no backfill, and no date extension after results are known. No result automatically authorizes paper-order promotion.

## Verification and remaining checks

The automated tests use mocked Coinbase and Alpaca evidence. They cover exact slot boundaries, stale/incomplete data, daily-target constancy, venue increments, minimum size, drift, cooldown, fee and friction accounting, chronological replay, pending/partial outcomes, restart idempotency, and immutable profile-scoped storage. **No prospective hourly performance result exists at registration.** The owner still needs to keep Coqui open for collection, inspect actual paper-account quote/asset reads and partial-fill evidence, verify complete-day coverage, and independently reconcile operational Alpaca orders in its API and dashboard. The candidate remains shadow-only unless a later owner-controlled promotion process authorizes a separately reviewed policy.
