# CSCV probability of backtest overfitting — 2026-08-09

## Method

Coqui implements Algorithm 2.3 from Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado, and
Zhu, [The Probability of Backtest Overfitting](https://escholarship.org/content/qt4w1110bb/qt4w1110bb.pdf).
The implementation consumes a synchronous matrix of after-cost daily candidate
returns from the development window only.

For an even pre-registered partition count `S`, it:

1. splits the observations into `S` contiguous equal-sized partitions;
2. forms every combination of `S/2` partitions as in-sample and uses its
   complement as out-of-sample;
3. selects the highest in-sample Sharpe candidate;
4. ranks that same candidate from worst to best out-of-sample;
5. converts its relative rank to the paper's logit; and
6. reports PBO as the fraction of logits below zero.

The pre-registration freezes `S` from 4 through 18. The planned study uses 16,
which produces 12,870 symmetric combinations. If development returns do not
divide evenly, Coqui deterministically excludes only the oldest remainder and
reports the exact count; this preserves equal partition dimensions and removes
post-result discretion. Exact performance ties receive their average rank, while
candidate selection ties resolve by stable grid order.

## Output and gate

The result includes:

- empirical probability of backtest overfitting;
- probability that the in-sample winner loses out-of-sample;
- the OOS-on-IS performance-degradation regression slope;
- mean IS and OOS performance; and
- every selected-candidate rank/logit observation.

The maximum acceptable PBO is also pre-registered. The proposed study uses
0.05, matching the paper's customary rejection threshold. Missing PBO or a PBO
above that limit fails adoption. This proposal can still be changed before the
real study is registered; it cannot be changed afterward.

## Boundaries and tests

CSCV uses all candidate returns in development but never receives final-holdout
returns. It complements rather than replaces the untouched chronological
holdout and DSR. PBO does not repair the unresolved historical trial count.

Tests cover a stable winner with PBO 0, partition-specific winners with PBO 1,
deterministic equal-partition remainder handling, insufficient data, and a
future perturbation proving holdout changes cannot alter PBO or selection.
