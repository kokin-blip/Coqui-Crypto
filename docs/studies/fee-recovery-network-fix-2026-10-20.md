## Result

- A quantity mismatch now triggers a full-history activity audit and fresh exact
  comparison even during an active pass. Published asset fees can resolve that
  mismatch through normal accounting before a pause is needed. If evidence is
  still insufficient, the pass remains blocked. Exceptions still require a pause.
- Fee-shaped discrepancies above either existing ceiling retain the exact
  differences and record the observed ask-price value, $25 ceiling, and 0.05%-of-
  equity ceiling. The recovery panel explains the block and unconfirmed fee
  attribution. Cash-only fees do not explain missing asset quantities.
- Position comparison errors now say “Alpaca position reconciliation mismatch.”
  A successful HTTP response is no longer mislabeled as a failed position read.
- Allowlisted transport codes survive the HTTP adapter, individual read-attempt
  journal, production observation wrapper, reconciliation error, status contract,
  activity timeline, and recovery panel. Messages distinguish DNS, socket,
  connectivity, timeout, and certificate failures. Short elapsed durations use
  milliseconds. Old records with no recorded cause remain unknown.
- Broken response streams are distinct from malformed JSON. GET reads remain
  bounded to two attempts within the same deadline; ambiguous submissions still
  get one attempt. No exception messages, URLs, headers, or credentials are
  copied into diagnostics. TLS verification remains enabled.

The $25 and 0.05% limits, exact Decimal accounting, broker account checks,
foreign-order rejection, fee-shape rounding band, full-audit requirement,
position stability, leases/fencing/deadlines, and manual Resume gate remain in
place. No synthetic fee is booked and no financial journal entry is rewritten.

The existing approximate $80.06 incident therefore **still blocks recovery**
until actual broker evidence explains the quantity gap. The patch improves fee
collection and the explanation; it does not turn a fee-shaped estimate into
accounting evidence or resolve an external network condition by assumption.
