## Decisions resolved during implementation

- **Future trial provenance:** migration 100 adds a ninth table, `prospective_trial_declarations_v1`, alongside the eight planned logical tables. Old immutable trial records cannot represent an unknown future dataset as verified evidence. New `prospective-declared` records have null dataset hashes, frozen protocol/registration references and declared search counts. The global registry includes them; `verifiedTrialCount` excludes them. Correlated capital books and resamples do not add independent alpha trials. Historical records/hashes are preserved.
- **Acquisition convention:** a frame marks the completed daily cut and separately supplies a later attainable quote, causal history and native evidence. Its decision executes at a strictly later acquired daily quote. Terminal development/final cuts value existing holdings without post-cut trading. The versioned protocol states this delayed model; it does not claim unknown first-tick or intrabar fills. Exposure controls use frozen owner-reviewed calibration weights and a warmup exposure reference, never future refitting.
- **Opening and partial history:** an opening holding must reconcile to its actual acquired market value, separately from tax basis. Unknown lot basis does not erase independently supported economic account value. Missing model continuity or requested endpoints withholds requested-window P&L/TWR/outcome, while separately dated observed balances and recorded counts remain visible. Missing daily marks are not compressed.
- **Corrections:** V1 freezes conservative quarantine rather than receipt-order truth selection. Provider corrections link prior captures, pause collection and append a superseding unavailable report when applicable. Old reports, search exposure, loss/H and shared consumption claims remain. No historical repair silently restores prospective eligibility.
- **Policy uncertainty:** joint stationary paths rebuild funded books, native quantities, fees, funding, yields and reserve transitions at all nine balances. The policy comparison uses the worst declared cost scenario rather than averaging away adverse doubled-cost results. Effective blocks, tail replications, resources, incomplete financial evidence and approximate conditional coverage remain explicit. Distribution/execution outcomes are separate from signal alpha selection; no optimal reserve share is inferred. Existing exposure risk residuals are explicitly recorded and withhold affected selection.
- **Qualification:** the P7 strategy namespace and next-daily-cut modeled per-turnover counterfactual differ from the old P6 TrendVol adapter. The proof requires positive compatible normalized bounds, current trial/coverage provenance, exact strategy/artifact/cost/horizon and an additional owner-reviewed capital/universe/funding/risk/protocol configuration. Review validity is integer-dated, at most one day and bounded by the design's fixed post-window expiry. Corrections/revocation/new trial exposure/expiry invalidate it. The existing P6 consumer supplies no new P7 configuration capability and remains blocked for that namespace until a separately reviewed operational adapter exists. No fake forward-edge result, champion activation, arm/Resume or financial authority is created.
- **Owner CLI:** an explicit reviewed acquisition-export directory is the initial source port. Its bounded current-slot JSON must match the frozen protocol; recording time is collector-owned. This is owner-attested acquisition evidence, not provider cryptographic proof. Exporter integrity/rights/availability/custody must be reviewed at H. The tool cannot assign host authority, migrate, create/switch profiles or select an installed database by default. See the [software protocol and workflow](../studies/capital-prospective-protocol-v1.md).

These are explicit implementation choices within retained requirements. They do not close an actual study, weaken historical 5% PBO/0.95 DSR or applicable 365-day/30-real-action gates, reinterpret P5 development results, or resolve P6 sale/connected ownership gates.
