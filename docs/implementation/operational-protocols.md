# Operational evidence protocols

These protocols do not authorize account access, provider activation, orders,
research transitions, actual-profile restoration or release publication.
Local fixtures prove semantics; they do not close V01/V04/V05/V07/V08/V09.

## WP-05 real-market attribution (COQ-6/7, V04)

Opt in on a dedicated approved session with `COQUI_PERFORMANCE_TRACE=1`.
`app.performance-trace` exposes a bounded 2,000-event ring, exact book level
counts, aggregation/update duration, correlated IPC reply identities, cumulative
process CPU/heap and event-loop delay. Preload emits bounded matching IPC marks.
Capture a DevTools Performance trace for renderer commits, chart updates,
input-to-paint, GC and scheduler/SQLite/news overlap. No retained market payload
is included in the diagnostic channel. Market-frame retention needs G02 rights.

Before capture record workload hash, source revision, installed build identity,
OS/hardware/power state, profile kind, symbol/depth, reconnect workload, trace
settings, cold/warm phases, sustained duration and exclusion rules. Record
receipt versus provider timestamps and instrument gaps; clock differences are
not automatically network latency. Report p50/p95/p99, payload/queue metrics
from the matching DevTools capture, not invented values. Distinguish fixture
replay from observed stutter. Absence of reproduction leaves COQ-6 open.
Compare any incremental depth prototype with exact full recomputation. Worker,
native rewrite or display projection requires separately reviewed attribution.
Disable tracing to roll back; do not alter authoritative research sequencing.

## WP-06 provider rights and identity (COQ-19–22, V05/G02)

Current collection configuration is unchanged. Budget remains $0. Record each
provider/publisher, terms version/URL and review date, approved entitlement,
title/snippet/body/URL/hash field permissions, expiry/deletion rules, derivatives,
quotation/export rights, owner and decision evidence. Unknown means denied for
new export permissions. Existing metadata availability is not a license grant.

Transport success, parsed candidates, retained observations, newly inserted
observations and canonical/eligible evidence are separate. Counts are trailing
24h scoped diagnostics; earlier runs without funnel evidence remain unknown.
Inserted observations do not prove independent articles or prospective relevance.
Review existing canonical registry and versioned alias configuration; unresolved
and ambiguous candidates remain visible. Do not add providers or buy quota to
repair mapping. Naturally due observations need approved configuration and rights.
A first page with zero articles does not establish comprehensive absent coverage.

Export authority is a separate immutable, field-scoped register in schema 93.
No grant exists by default, including for report/decision/outcome hashes.
The approved manifest binds one exact artifact and profile, allowed fields,
review/expiry times, terms URL/version/evidence hash and owner approval hash.
Decision/outcome summaries additionally require G07 authority evidence; each
outcome has its own permission rather than inheriting a decision's grant.
Latest deny/expiry/future-time uncertainty blocks use. Clones exclude grants.
Permission is rechecked after native destination choice before writing.

Only after separate G02/G07 approval, an administrator can record the reviewed
manifest with `node scripts/record-news-export-permission.mjs --database EXISTING_SCHEMA_93_DB
--manifest APPROVED_PERMISSION.json --confirm-owner-reviewed`. This CLI never
migrates a DB, buys rights, activates collection or verifies that a claimed license
is legally sufficient. It records the owner's evidence; fabricated hashes/terms
are not operational proof. There is no renderer grant command. No actual grant
was recorded during implementation. Raw article fields are never exportable here.
