## 1. Delivery boundary and decisions

Deliver a finite, reproducible **offline development experiment capability**, plus an evidence-qualified development evaluation when adequate permitted inputs exist. Tooling acceptance and actual research completion are separate tracker states. Neither requires a profitable winner; `none`, unavailable and negative outcomes are first-class results. No existing campaign/default is changed.

- Initially compare only two new structural hypotheses: simple absolute trend/cash and buffered momentum rotation. Keep the current allocator, constrained hold/passive, cash and training-calibrated exposure controls.
- Separate structural, execution, volatility/allocation and reserve questions. Declare every arm, contrast, funding/cost/overhead case, diagnostic and selection rule before opening performance inputs. No unrestricted Cartesian optimizer or per-balance hindsight winner.
- Run the actual book at $50/$100/$250/$500/$1,000/$2,500/$5,000/$10,000/$100,000. The last is diagnostic. These share paths and are not nine independent alpha discoveries.
- Preserve `capital-aware-replay-v1`, P1/P2/P3 numerical semantics, archived reports, source identities and legacy studies. A new `capital-contest-v1` identity owns new decision/execution/inference semantics. An explicitly tested internal kernel extraction is permitted; changing a v1 result is not.
- Default research seed: one 120-day trailing-return lookback and weekly signal review; rotation top one, rank buffer two, 30-day volatility scoring and equal selected weights. These are finite hypotheses inherited from source/research, not optimized values, current settings or recommendations. All parameters are explicit in a run manifest; no omitted value inherits future defaults.
- Freeze the same risk policy across capital sizes. Cap targets downward and keep the remainder cash. Never enlarge a position, lower a hard stop, force extra trades or change signals to make a small account feasible.
- Execution uses an inactive empty research profile, explicit local inputs/output, no provider ports and no app Run command. Main only reads saved artifacts. P6 owns optional reviewed paper integration; P7 owns fresh prospective registration/qualification.

No product decision is required to implement contracts, compatibility, causal adapters and synthetic fixtures. Real data eligibility, study choices and user sacrifice preferences are required manifest inputs before a market evaluation, not facts inferred in this plan. No market backtest, account inspection, prospective start or study registration occurred here.

## 4. Causal policy and execution design

### Preserve v1; add a state-aware kernel only under a compatibility gate

Extract a narrow internal `capital-replay-kernel.ts` around the existing event phases. Keep the public P4 functions and v1 schemas/IDs/outputs on their fixed-tape compatibility path. Before changing that path, archive/verify full-result hashes for flat, deposits, received fees, partial fills, unknown flows and reserve cycles across all nine sizes, then retain all 65 P4 tests and numerical/P0–P3 fixtures. The extraction must reproduce v1 JSON, including event, scope and report IDs, exactly for unchanged inputs. A mismatch blocks the extraction; do not update goldens to make it pass. Current dependency manifests may legitimately change; historical reports/registrations are not migrated.

New `capital-contest-v1` calls a pure `DecisionSource` and `ExecutionIntentPolicy` with hash-bound definitions. It cannot receive a provider, storage writer or arbitrary executable plugin. Decision context contains only causally available completed history, membership and a book cut whose journal sequence predates the decision. Execution context contains the already frozen target and current supported execution budget/quotes. It cannot fit a signal or revise an earlier decision.

Schedule decision events at their actual declared time **between daily open and close**, not at the next execution open. A deposit, fill, fee or interest credit that occurs after the decision cannot affect that decision's held basket or features. In particular, the later day's closing mark and a next-open deposit cannot become the earlier rotation input. Book quantities/cash can be known without inventing a decision-time price; if a required valuation is absent, return unavailable. Initial incumbent state is the explicit planned opening book (cash by seed); record that convention rather than claiming a pre-origin observed balance. First attainable execution is strictly after decision and availability using the existing timing convention.

Signals run at a frozen weekly anchor; independent daily risk checks and available execution cuts remain active. A defensive exit is distinguished from scheduled discretionary rotation and from stale/insufficient history. No interpolation, same-close hindsight trade, future membership or missing-day compression. Long-only spot, no leverage/shorts/stablecoin substitution.

### Policy adapters

- T1: use the single completed trailing return; positive means retain its declared base exposure, nonpositive means fiat cash. No relative tilt, hidden defensive residual, volatility sizing or ensemble in T1. Require full dated lookback; missing history is not a negative signal. V isolates the later volatility modifier.
- R1: apply the existing positive absolute filter, risk-adjusted ranking and explicit buffer to the **actual held basket at decision time**. Stable instrument-ID tie order and dust/incumbent rules are frozen. Do not treat an unfilled intention as a held position. If fewer than three eligible/ranked candidates make buffering ineffective, expose that condition and its observation count; do not manufacture a replacement effect.
- L: snapshot one concrete existing allocator family/configuration and all caps/source hashes. Do not use `auto` to select a historical winner or inherit subsequently changed defaults. Preserve the negative TrendVol finding rather than calling this control validated.
- P/H: same instrument universe, opening holdings, flows, fees, native legality and applicable exposure caps. H buys once as declared; P adjusts on the declared cadence. Cash is deliberate inactivity. Failures to execute remain blocked demand.
- XT/XR: fit only a scalar exposure level to each candidate's **prior training** exposure, then freeze a capped passive cash/asset mix for the next validation interval. Charge its rebalancing/entry costs. No full-validation exposure normalization or retrospective beta matching. Report remaining exposure/concentration differences; this comparator does not establish causal alpha.

All adapters clip targets to the stricter per-asset/account/invested caps and leave unused weight cash; no upward normalization. Entry/order/turnover limits still apply independently through P2. There is one rule set across sizes; feasibility may differ. Closed-loop decisions may differ across books and are honestly labeled as such.

### Isolate E/R questions

For E, freeze each capital's reference-policy target stream and risk-exit reasons before comparing execution modes. Both modes receive identical targets, costs, flow schedule, timing and P2 final constraints. A/C differences are in discretionary intention generation; mandatory exits cannot be suppressed by a cost or no-trade screen. Pending operations retain obligations and native dust remains residual risk.

The A adapter preserves the documented legacy intention conventions where compatible; its new identity explicitly reports changed timing/valuation/native/cost assumptions. It is **not** the original $100,000/four-hour frozen study. Keep that old study untouched as historical evidence. C uses the existing asymmetric change/routine band concepts and adjusts toward a frozen band edge; this is a heuristic exposure/cost trade-off, not expected-profit proof. Do not implement partial adjustment as a fake half fill with an automatically pending remainder.

For R, use the same frozen target stream for all shares. Reserve movement changes active capital and actual orders, not the reference's prediction. On a resampled market path, regenerate the causal reference stream on that path before freezing it for the matched arms. A separately declared closed-loop end-to-end comparison can study reserve feedback into future holdings; do not confuse it with a same-target ablation.

R reuses strict P3 economic/realized/cash/floor caps, prospective H/cycle state, rounding/residue, claims reconciliation and fixed-request witness. Seed examples may use a 10%-of-fixed-base growth trigger, weekly review, $1 batch and 80%-of-opening retained preference solely as explicit synthetic research values, not user defaults or optimal choices. Dollar/balance triggers remain supported and preserved, without being silently added to the initial search. Wins remain advisory.

If R cannot establish the operating request, realized profit or available cash, report unavailable/deferred; a cash-only objective cannot be substituted because trades failed. Release and confirmed modeled receipt are distinct, preserve H and never imply a real payment. The economic-cap review uses a different, simulation-only identity, retains net economic/cash/capital/fee/unknown-activity safeguards and never fabricates tax basis. Compare the extra turnover/exposure required for a strictly profit-realizing sale with a cash allocation; if neither meets the declared objective, record `none / sale-funded reserve unavailable`. No automatic sale-funded behavior enters P3, P4 or a paper account.

No automatic permission downgrade to “synthetic.” If existing metadata cannot establish an allowed development interval, fail closed. Any separate owner-led review of already exposed research is not this runner's authority.

Require raw decimal execution prices, complete UTC alignment and provider identity; a numerical signal panel must be derived from the same hashed rows. Preserve missing values and listing/delisting/status observations. No tail alignment, invalid-price zero or silently removed crash period. A fixed conditional universe can be reported as an explicitly assumed diagnostic; it cannot be called survivor-free historical eligibility or qualify the wider-universe claim. Missing membership/rules/costs produce reasoned unavailable states for affected comparisons. This pass did not verify any real archive or installed profile.

Freeze chronological training/validation cuts and independent warmup, label purge/embargo and complete-date asset groups. New P5 uses expanding prior-only training, never `purgedKFold` alone (it can include future training rows). Fitted artifacts bind training end, all label-maturity times, calibration interval and effective time. ML, if later admitted, needs training then disjoint calibration then validation; an unavailable artifact abstains and counts coverage/fallback rather than earning a result. No news/on-chain/attention predictor is enabled without timestamp/provenance and incremental-value evidence.

Do not erase capital/basis/H at fold boundaries. A continuous walk-forward book carries holdings, fees and reserve state forward; fold metrics are cuts of that ledger. A separately reset fold-book diagnostic must be labeled separately and cannot be stitched into a continuous wealth curve. All variants and the predeclared choice rule see identical fold boundaries. Missing rows block dependent inference rather than reducing its sample opportunistically.

### Separate finite trial history from replication

Keep candidate/parameter/model/execution/reserve/universe/funding/cost hypotheses, deterministic retries and bootstrap replications as separate dimensions. Enumerate all financial cells, but do not claim nine capital rows or 2,000 bootstrap paths are independent alpha trials. Record any newly inspected diagnostic that informs a new hypothesis as human-guided search. Retain failed/interrupted attempts, prior searches and input corrections. Global registry states remain `complete`, `conservative-upper-bound` or `known-lower-bound`; the last withholds DSR eligibility. Do not pad an observed Sharpe sample with invented zero trials or call a new local inventory globally complete.

Reuse `deflatedSharpeForTrials` and synchronous CSCV after metric/time checks, preserving the existing sample/partition limitations and disclosed trimmed observations. Do not mix reserve-share outcomes into an alpha PBO contest. Keep each frozen stricter requirement: no replacement of 5% PBO by a general 10% rule, no shortening applicable 365-observation/30-action gates, no fabricated significance when the sample/history is sparse. P5 outputs no qualification approval even when diagnostics are available.

### Paired joint-path inference

Initial method: seeded stationary joint-row blocks, with the entire asset row and its known observation masks sampled together. Reuse only `stationaryBootstrapIndices`/PRNG. Reconstruct decimal overnight/intraday price moves under a new scenario identity and causal availability; no OHLC inference of spread, queues or stop order. Resample supported contiguous panels only; gaps, status changes and delistings also receive explicit original-calendar stress scenarios rather than being cleaned into zero. Report stationarity/fixed-universe assumptions and boundary-join limitations.

For every path, regenerate signals, prior-only calibration, actual per-size execution, fees, flows, P3 reserve decisions and unit NAV **inside** that path. All compared arms share its sampled indices. Funding calendars are frozen external schedules, not chosen from the sampled return winners. Never shuffle a withdrawn equity/return curve and append payouts afterward. Bootstrap replicas are model-conditioned uncertainty, not new market history or fresh prospective evidence.

Use a transparent paired **Bonferroni basic bootstrap interval** over a frozen finite primary contrast family: for observed difference theta and path differences theta*, use `[2 theta − q(1−alpha/(2K)), 2 theta − q(alpha/(2K))]`. K includes every primary candidate/comparator/cost/outcome contrast used by that decision; unrelated currencies/units use separate declared families. Report approximate conditional coverage, not an exact finite-sample guarantee. Require supported endpoints/daily NAV as appropriate, minimum declared history/effective blocks and at least 20 nominal tail replications at each used quantile; insufficient resolution produces unavailable, never a zero-width or unadjusted interval. Resource preflight must account for the resulting replication budget before data performance is opened. Do not extend a run because a p-value was disappointing.

For a manageable initial selection family, freeze a primary small-capital region **before outcomes**, with fixed weights over that region (the $100,000 control has zero primary weight). Weighted cohort excess is one paired statistic, not many independent accounts. Also require every member's declared feasibility and pointwise cost/drawdown safeguards; averaging cannot conceal a failed capital cell. All nine rows remain reported, including outside-region failures. Changing the region after results is a new trial, not a discovered universal minimum. An example K=12 primary family needs at least 9,600 paths for the tail-resolution rule; an example K=54 needs 43,200. Exact K and budgets are manifest facts, not hardcoded assumptions. Individual-row diagnostic intervals cannot be used to pick different winners.

A faster resampling-based stepdown method is not added merely to obtain attractive significance. Any future method needs its own version, calibration, multiplicity and source review. On current hardware a large full-path job may be impractical; run preflight estimates time/storage and refuse or checkpoint explicitly, retaining the whole planned inventory. No CI is invented from a convenient partial set.

### Deterministic choice or none

Freeze one global selection rule for T1/R1: required evidence/metrics and trial-history diagnostics must be available; the declared primary paired excess bounds versus constrained passive and training-calibrated exposure controls must be positive; fold/critical-cost/drawdown/feasibility conditions must hold; and the manifest's stricter source gates remain satisfied. Rank surviving candidates by their worst declared primary lower bound, with a predeclared simplicity/ID tie rule. No survivor means `none`, with exact reasons. Nonprimary statistics never override a failure. An unqualified numerical leader may be displayed only as descriptive evidence, not an alternate selected candidate.

R has a separate required objective: allowed total user-net-outcome sacrifice versus reinvestment, required downside/floor behavior, and required amount reserved/returned. These fields must be supplied before evaluation; synthetic examples can use a 5% sacrifice solely to test plumbing. Compute account equity, active equity, reserved/tax/pending amounts, confirmed modeled receipt, contribution recovery, external expenses, costs, time underwater, worst supported rolling outcomes and forgone upside. Normalize only as declared; account-plus-receipts is a dollar outcome, not CAGR or after-tax personal wealth. Principal and personal-tax outflows are not profit distributions. A lower drawdown alone is not improved alpha. Return none/unavailable when objective evidence is inadequate.

## 6. Minimal targeted primary-source checks

No broad strategy literature repeat or new venue-rate claim was needed. The phase-specific statistical questions were checked against primary material:

- Politis–Romano's stationary bootstrap resamples random-length blocks under weakly dependent stationary assumptions. This supports reuse of the index mechanism, not a claim that Coqui crash regimes satisfy its assumptions or that a close-only helper is an execution simulator. [Published paper](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1994.10476870), [paper copy](https://users.ssc.wisc.edu/~behansen/718/Politis%20Romano.pdf).
- DSR explicitly depends on selection/search information and non-normality; source registration completeness must survive the new experiment. [Author preprint](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf).
- CSCV assesses selection overfitting; it does not create untouched data. [Authors' PBO preprint](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf).
- Dependence-aware multiple-test improvements require methodological conditions. The first implementation keeps a fixed transparent Bonferroni family rather than claiming to implement the full Romano–Wolf procedure. [Authors' paper](https://www.econ.uzh.ch/dam/jcr:ffffffff-935a-b0d6-ffff-ffffd823d949/jasa.pdf).

The capital-aware reconstruction, choice/cohort objective and resource rules above are Coqui design inferences, not paper-reported performance or an endorsed optimal setting. Original conflicting trend/volatility/technical-rule findings, negative TrendVol decision/PBO 28.60%, consumed data and source-access limitations remain unchanged.
