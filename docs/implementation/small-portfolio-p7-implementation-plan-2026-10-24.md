## 1. Boundary and decisions

P7 provides a separately versioned prospective study lifecycle, capital-aware shadow observations, truthful coverage, sealed final evidence and qualification decisions. It does not make the P5 development contest confirmatory, enroll a P6 book, authorize orders, enable sales, register anything automatically or create a payment capability.

Use **prospective shadow simulation first**, isolated from the P6 account ledger. Capture genuine future market evidence under an owner-approved protocol and advance independent modeled books. Trading-signal, execution and reserve questions remain separate. Model fills/costs/receipts stay modeled, even when their input quotes are observed. Actual paper executions may later be linked as a distinct evidence class only under their existing authorization and applicable P6 gates.

No owner answer is required to build disabled contracts/storage/projections and synthetic lifecycle tests. Before a real registration, the owner and design reviewer must approve the exact study, future windows, rights, resource budget, evidence classes, power/minimum requirements and qualification mapping. Values are required fields, not silently supplied defaults. No proposed calendar dates are assigned here.

For a P5 hypothesis, actual registration/adoption requires the complete P5-H development record and frozen choice/objectives. P7 tooling cannot manufacture that record. A genuinely separate study would require its own explicit design review; it is not a route around P5-H. G/H are not prerequisites to simulated cash-only shadow observations, but remain prerequisites to their sale or connected operational claims.

## 4. Observation and financial semantics

The collection port has clock, explicitly registered protocol, bounded read acquisition, immutable storage and pure model transitions only. It has no execution service, profitability permit, broker mutation, registration, champion or transfer port.

Capture facts retain slot/effective/available/observed/recorded times separately, source batch/hash, native rules, fees/cost assumptions and coverage/failure reasons. A slot is prospective only when acquisition/availability/lateness satisfy the approved protocol. A historical bar downloaded after a missed slot is labeled retrospective and cannot fill its prospective count. Warmup is development/training evidence and contributes no prospective duration.

Use a stable registration/slot/acquisition operation identity and one host-fenced single-flight collector. Obtain data outside a DB transaction; validate current profile, registration, host generation and approved collector state again inside one bounded append transaction. Exact repeats are idempotent. Conflicting captures are retained and quarantine the slot; no receipt-order correction. A provider correction links the old fact and produces a revised report; whether it restores eligibility follows the predeclared correction rule, never hindsight.

Persist the captured frame, all declared cell transitions/statuses, cumulative input manifest and acknowledgment atomically. A cell can be infeasible/unavailable while others remain independently reportable. A partial batch cannot count as complete matched evidence. Keep pending orders, fee-posting liabilities, reservations, H/cycle/base, positions, basis, turnover and last supported valuation in each versioned modeled book. Do not replay an account curve by multiplying its return by opening balance.

Reuse pure `createReplayBook`, fill/fee/native-budget/lot/reserve and P1 metric primitives. The current whole-tape kernel is not an incremental persistence contract: introduce a small versioned transition/checkpoint adapter and prove parity with frozen P4/P5 fixtures before using it. Prefer composition of existing pure functions; if extracting a shared internal transition is necessary, preserve old exports, IDs, numeric outputs and source goldens. Do not bypass `offline_development` validation by forging a P4 spec.

Observed quotes/rules and modeled fill assumptions remain separately attributed. OHLC supplies no unknown intrabar sequence, spread, queue or favorable execution. Decisions use only completed available data and the first attainable later execution; store unavailable or frozen conservative treatment for ambiguity. Fee estimates never become actual postings. New P7 shadow events never enter the canonical **verified account** ledger or the live P6 cash balance. A simulated contribution/outflow/reserve/release is explicitly simulated.

Report full managed equity and active sleeve separately. Count reserve/tax/pending subsets once. Use P1 cash-flow-correct performance with metric-specific unavailable results: deposits/withdrawals/basis-only edits do not create return; missing marks do not become zero or compressed daily samples. Include dollar user-net outcome, remaining capital, modeled receipts, principal recovery, overhead, turnover, concentration, blocked intentions, risk residuals, floor breaches, time underwater and forgone compounding. A reserve is not a receipt or crash guarantee.

Action counts have explicit classes: scheduled decisions, abstentions, infeasible intentions, modeled friction-bearing rebalances, real paper fills/settled episodes, reserve assessments and review-only win triggers. Deduplicate episodes; partial fills and nine correlated books do not inflate independent actions. A legacy requirement for actual cost-bearing actions cannot be fulfilled by modeled counts. If applicable prerequisites prohibit actual orders, remain insufficient; do not bypass qualification to manufacture evidence.

## 5. Coverage and sealed final evidence

Compute expected completed slots from immutable registration windows and current trusted time, with terminal valuation and source-completion deadlines; future slots are pending. Show actual first/last eligible acquisition, expected/accepted/invalid/missing/late/conflicting slots, complete matched dates and action counts **by evidence class**. Uptime and days since install are not coverage. Pause, outage, host loss and missed wake retain gaps and the original clock. A replay of a gap cannot start a replacement clock.

Before final claim, a read API may expose final **non-outcome metadata** only: expected/received slot counts, source/identity eligibility, failure categories and custody hashes/byte counts needed for integrity. It must not select/deserialise final book balances, positions, returns, fill directions, modeled turnover, action counts derived from outcomes, rankings or diagnostic payloads. If an eligible-action minimum itself reveals an outcome, it remains sealed until the authorized evaluator checks it; pre-claim readiness reports that requirement as sealed/unverified. Data-quality eligibility counts may be visible only when their definition is outcome-independent.

Enforce this in SQL/repository projection, not renderer filtering. Development report queries end before the final boundary. Raw export, lineage, backups displayed in the app, worker snapshots, logs, previews, telemetry and general research views cannot provide an alternate final-result read. Backups retain opaque facts, but importing/copying them grants no evaluator capability. No claim of secrecy from the local machine owner is made: owner inspection/exposure records disqualify confirmatory freshness.

Final service sequence:

1. Verify exact owner-authorized request/instance/runtime, complete development result and global trial inventory, explicit immutable candidate-or-none freeze, final window elapsed, coverage metadata and known exposure/consumption history. Missing provenance/unknown prior access blocks. Freeze `none` terminates with no final body read or claim.
2. For a candidate, persist an attempt and call the existing shared `claimIntegrityHoldout` **before loading any final outcome**. Compare normalized instrument identities and intervals across namespaces/providers/aliases where evidence overlaps; unresolved aliases block. The claim and its P7 linkage must be atomic within that repository's transaction owner. Do not nest its current `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`; extend its internal append hook or transaction-aware wrapper without changing legacy semantics.
3. After the durable claim, an evaluator-scoped capability reads only that frozen candidate and declared controls/cells, verifies stored cut hashes and applies the registered statistics/criteria. It cannot explore alternatives, retune, add trials or open other final intervals.
4. Store an immutable passed/failed/insufficient report or interrupted/failed consumed attempt. A claim is never deleted on a crash. Identical completed report retrieval is read-only; an unfinished/failed attempt cannot be rerun under a new ID. Recover only already-durable result/acknowledgment facts; any further evaluation needs explicit governance and genuinely unexposed evidence, not a reset.

Use the authoritative trial/claim registry, not a new empty profile's apparent history. A missing imported legacy/consumption inventory is unavailable, not proof of freshness. Same-profile restore retains claims/clock; stale continuity requires explicit reconciliation. Duplicates preserve originating identities/exposure and archive collector bindings. No registration, elapsed evidence, qualification or authority is renewed by a copy.

## 6. Development evaluation and qualification

Use matched complete-date unit-NAV returns, declared passive/cash/exposure controls and the same source/funding/cost assumptions. Retain chronological fitting, purge/embargo and causal calibration for applicable ML hypotheses. Evaluate the finite family jointly, count prior/failed/interrupted/human-guided trials, and preserve lower-bound/upper-bound/global-complete distinctions. Missing trial history withholds DSR eligibility. Bootstrap replicates, balances and policy arms are not independent alpha discoveries.

Reuse P5 paired/path/inference machinery where its assumptions apply, with a separately versioned prospective input adapter. Preserve approximate-bootstrap limitations, effective samples, sparse-action insufficiency, stress outcomes, rolling/drawdown and cost/parameter stability. No new statistical threshold or scientific optimal share is inferred here.

Gate applicability is an immutable reviewed design input. Retain any applicable PBO ≤5%, DSR ≥0.95, 365-complete-day/30-action and stricter registered gates; the plan cannot silently replace them with another study's generic rules. Different old designs are not merged into one sample. A new sparse design needs explicit power/semantics review and sufficient evidence; it must not force more trades or pick a convenient deadline.

Separate decisions:

- **Development choice:** conditional candidate or none, exact capital support region and selection weights. No per-size winner or universal minimum account claim.
- **Distribution-policy assessment:** predeclared cash-return/reserve/downside objective and allowable outcome sacrifice versus reinvestment; lower exposure/drawdown is not improved alpha. Strict realized-cap cash-only P6 semantics remain; P5 economic-cash/full-exit diagnostics do not authorize G.
- **Final evidence assessment:** passed, failed, insufficient or consumed/unknown, with each required metric independently available/unavailable and its input hash.
- **Paper configuration acceptance:** an explicit owner/independent-review record pins exact strategy, capital/flow support region, universe, native/cost/risk/fee/evidence scope, horizon and validity limits. No champion change, P6 arm, order or live permission is a side effect.

Add a typed `QualifiedPaperEvidenceV1` and `readApplicablePaperQualification` only after exact semantics are reviewed. It must support the same gross-lower-bound-per-turnover, net cost gate, horizon/normalization, source-profile and current configuration expected by execution. A total return or reserve-wealth figure cannot be converted into that lower bound. Unsupported horizons, costs, evidence classes or normalization stay unavailable. Preserve the existing forward-edge tables/binder/reader and their legacy behavior. Adapt P6/execution consumers explicitly to the new proof type only in the reviewed qualification slice, retaining all original guards; never mint a fake forward-edge result. Actual P6 use still requires its separate manual authorization and current financial/host/lease proofs.
